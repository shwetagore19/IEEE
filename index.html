About ICETEC 2027

Conference Overview

A premier international forum advancing emerging technologies across engineering, artificial intelligence, robotics, and next-generation computing.

The IEEE International Conference on Emerging Technologies in Engineering & Computing (ICETEC 2027) provides an esteemed international platform for global academicians, researchers, scientists, and industry practitioners to present and discuss breakthroughs in frontier engineering paradigms.

Hosted by the prestigious K. K. Wagh Institute of Engineering Education & Research, Nashik, under the aegis of IEEE Mumbai Section, ICETEC 2027 promotes interdisciplinary dialogue, industry-academia linkages, and sustainable technological solutions to real-world global challenges.

The conference follows a double-blind peer-review mechanism. Accepted and presented high-quality research papers will be submitted for inclusion into the IEEE Xplore Digital Library.

Key Conference Metrics

  • 50+ Research Topics From Edge AI to 6G Networks and Quantum Computing
  • 200+ Submissions Expected Global participation from leading universities and research labs

Submission Standards

  • Papers must present novel, original, unpublished research contributions.
  • Strict IEEE double-column format (Maximum 6 pages including figures & references).
  • Must be written in English with high grammatical standards.
  • Double-blind review process: Manuscripts must not include author names/affiliations.
  • Submitted electronically in PDF format via Microsoft CMT conference portal.
  • At least one author of every accepted paper must register and present the work.
